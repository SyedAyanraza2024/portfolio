Internships

My overall experience at internships.

Three internships in three organisations: .NET development at PIA, data analysis at SSGC, and web development at Engro Fertilizers.

Sep – Oct 2026

Legacy System Modernization & Web Application Development | EFERT (Engro Fertilizers)
  • Revived a dormant enterprise web application that had remained inactive for approximately a decade, digitizing previously hardcoded valve maintenance records and restoring an established workflow for the Instrumentation team.
  • Took ownership of modernizing an existing legacy system rather than rebuilding it from scratch, reverse-engineering interconnected pages, tracing dependencies, and navigating unfamiliar application logic to identify and reconstruct the required functionality.
  • Migrated and integrated the existing database using Apache, MySQL, on XAMPP, preserving historical records and supporting the transition from manual record-keeping to a structured digital platform.
  • Rebuilt key application interfaces and workflows using HTML5, JavaScript, Bootstrap, Node.js, and Express.js, with a focus on usability, maintainability, and a more accessible experience for employees.
  • Addressed legacy-system security concerns by modernizing the application architecture and improving its readiness for safer data handling, with particular attention to risks such as SQL injection.
  • Restored a previously discontinued operational workflow, providing the Instrumentation team with a dedicated web-based solution for managing valve records and maintenance-related information as an alternative to relying solely on SAP-based processes.
Jul – Aug 2026

Data Analytics & ERP Intern | Sui Southern Gas Company Limited (SSGC)
  • Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on large-scale datasets provided by the assigned mentor, examining data patterns, identifying trends, and generating insights to support data-driven decision-making.
  • Participated in a rotation-based internship across departments, gaining exposure to enterprise data analytics, business processes, and organizational workflows.
  • Explored the Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) module within Oracle E-Business Suite (Oracle EBS), learning asset management processes, handling correspondence related to Internal Requisitions (IRs), and understanding request-based workflows.
  • Gained practical exposure to enterprise resource planning (ERP), asset lifecycle management, and the integration of data-driven operations with corporate business processes.
Aug – Sep 2025

PIA Fire Safety Management System

ASP.NET, .NET Framework, C#, SQL Server, AngularJS, HTML, CSS

Developed a centralized web application for Pakistan International Airlines (PIA) to digitize fire safety records and maintenance data, modernizing record management for the Security and Fire Safety teams. Contributed to front-end development, backend integration, and system testing, delivering a structured digital solution to improve data accessibility, record organization, and operational efficiency.
